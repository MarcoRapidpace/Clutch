✅ What's working

The welcome email is the star

32.0% of new players open it and 11.8% click, and 37% of those who open go on to click. It is the highest-intent moment in the whole journey, and it puts Racing front and centre.

The deposit ladder is read

34.3% of post-deposit emails are opened. The 4th-deposit loyalty email opens at 53.1% and clicks at 30.6%, the best of any message in the account.

Win-backs are getting attention

The High-risk win-back pair opens at 26.9% and the Declining check-in at 27.3%: players who had drifted are still opening Clutch mail.

The weekly lossback lands

The Monday free-bet emails opened at 60% (12 Oct) and 47% (19 Oct). A predictable weekly reward is clearly something players look out for.

Big-event promos reach players fast

Fifteen campaigns went out in under three weeks, from the Champions League and NFL to CS, Valorant and UFC, each built and sent around the fixture.

A clean, healthy list

Zero spam reports, zero unsubscribes and one hard bounce across almost 10,000 emails. The sending reputation is in great shape for growth.

🚀 Where we grow next

Each idea builds on something that already works. The first is a direct answer to the growth coming from ZED Racing.

1. A Racing-first journey for ZED players Built, ready to launch

ZED players already see Racing in every lifecycle email. The next step is content written for them, so a player who arrived for the horses gets a journey that speaks their language from their first race. All three emails are built, using only what is live on the Clutch Racing site and in the ZED Racing light paper.

Who it targets. A ZED Racing – New Racers segment, built on data the CRM already receives: a Bet on Racing (vendor zed-casino) by a player in their first 14 days. Each player enters once, at their first race.

“Getting started with ZED Racing”: three emails over the first week

  • Day 1 · Welcome to the Track. A race every 80 seconds with 8 runners, live in 3D or 2D. Win, Place, Multi, Trifecta, Supafecta and Dutch bets, how to read a race card, pre-betting and free cancellation.
  • Day 4 · Build Your Stable. Packs of five horses (60% Common, 30% Strong, 10% Elite), stats that reveal themselves as horses race, and a stable name, crest and colours of your own.
  • Day 8 · Climb the Leaderboards. The Racehorses leaderboard (top 10 share the weekly pool, 25% to first, plus a season pool) and the Wagering leaderboard (daily, weekly and monthly, no horse needed).

A Racing season calendar for a ZED Racing – Active Racers segment (a Racing bet in the last 30 days), timed to the dates on the Racing site. Season 2 runs 14 Oct – 11 Nov, with weekly leaderboards resetting every Wednesday: weekly leaderboard winners each Wednesday, a “last week of pack sales” reminder before sales close at the end of week 3, and a season finale on 11 November as the next season begins.

2. Two new journeys, built and ready

Launching next

Both lead on Racing and reuse proven copy, so they extend the programme without new design work:

  • RND Day 10: players who registered ten days ago and have not deposited yet get “Racing Is Live, and Your Account Is Ready”, with card & bank deposits to remove the crypto barrier. Around 80 players a day reach this point.
  • Inactive 1 (soft churn): one-time depositors who have been away for two to four weeks get a friendly “It’s been a while” catch-up led by Racing, Run It Back Monday and combo boosts.

3. Cross-sell between Racing and Sportsbook

Quick win

Racing players are already betting on fast, odds-based markets, which is exactly the Sportsbook audience. Add Racing players to the Superboost and Combo Boost sends, and add a Racing tile to the weekly lossback's “What's on this week” so sportsbook players discover ZED.

4. Give the welcome email an even bigger stage

Quick win

At 32% opens and 11.8% clicks it is the highest-intent email in the account. Test one subject line per week and a larger first-deposit button; a one-point lift here is worth more than an extra campaign.

5. A VIP and loyalty journey

Biggest untapped value

Active bettors (313) and Low risk / loyal (194) are the most valuable players in the database and currently hear from Clutch through campaigns only. A short thank-you journey with a reload offer and early access to new features (Racing seasons included) would protect that revenue. The segments already exist and refresh automatically.

6. Keep the weekly fixtures protected

Build on a winner

The Monday lossback is the best-opening send in the calendar. Keeping it, and a regular Superboost, on fixed days gives players a reason to come back on a predictable rhythm.

📌 In short

The foundations are strong. Nine journeys run on their own, the welcome and deposit emails perform well above industry benchmarks, the list is spotless, and every new player, including the 2,585 who joined Onboarding in the last 30 days, is looked after automatically from minute one. With ZED Racing bringing in a new wave of players, the next phase is about giving them Racing-first content on top of a programme that already works.
